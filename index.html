Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions clients ask most often.

Who is a retainer suitable for?

Small and medium businesses without a full HR team, companies entering Singapore, and organisations growing quickly and needing HR support on a regular basis.

How does it work?

Get in touch through the contact form. We arrange a consultation to understand your needs, agree on the right package and payment terms, and then begin.

Can I change my plan?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade from one month to the next. Most arrangements have a minimum commitment of three months.

Will I have a dedicated contact?

Yes. You will have one main point of contact who gets to know your business and manages your requests.

When am I billed?

Fees are invoiced in advance for the following month.

Are there any extra costs?

There is no set-up fee. Software, tools, and any additional services are quoted separately if needed.
